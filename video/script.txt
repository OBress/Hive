[hook]
Software is now built by people and AI agents, side by side.

[problem]
But every agent works alone: its own tab, its own chat, its own branch. Nobody sees what the others are doing. Decisions get lost, and work collides.

[intro +0.9]
Hive fixes that: one live task graph where your whole team and its agents build together.

[plan]
Ana describes what she wants: a link-shortening app. Hive's AI breaks it into a plan of tasks.

[sync]
Her whole team sees the same plan, live.

[run]
One click, and AI agents start writing the code, while everyone watches.

[steer]
Ben tells the agent to count unique visitors, not total clicks. It replies, and changes its approach.

[takeover]
Maya pauses the agent and edits the code herself. Then the agent picks up where she left off.

[saved]
Nothing gets lost: every chat, agent step and edit is saved to MongoDB, so anyone can catch up.

[outro +2.6]
Hive. The collaborative workspace where people and AI agents build software together.
